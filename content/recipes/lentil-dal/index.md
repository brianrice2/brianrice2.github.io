## Ingredients

- 1 cup dry green/brown lentils
- 1 can crushed, diced tomatoes
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 inch fresh ginger, or 1/2 tsp ground
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1-2 tsp ground cumin
- 1-2 tsp garam masala
- 2 Tbsp butter
- Salt, pepper, and milk or cream to taste

## Preparation

Cover the lentils with a couple inches of water and boil until tender. Strain. In the meantime, prepare the spices.

Sauté the onion, adding the garlic after a few minutes, and bloom the spices after. Then add the rest of the ingredients.

Bring to a boil and then cover and simmer for 30 minutes until thick and creamy, not watery.

Garnish with milk or cream, cilantro, etc. Serve with rice or naan.
