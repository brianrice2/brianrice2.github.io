+++
title = "Peeling Pearl Onions"
+++

- Cut off the _root_ ends of the pearl onions. The more you cut, the easier it will be to squeeze them out later on, but obviously don't just cut them in half.
- Prepare an ice bath
- Boil some water
- Add the pearl onions and boil for ~90 seconds
- Transfer them to the ice bath
- Pull and squeeze out by the stem end
